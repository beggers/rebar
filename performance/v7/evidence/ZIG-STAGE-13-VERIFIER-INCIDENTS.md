# Zig Stage 13: verification and isolation incidents

## Read-only reviewer role confusion

An additional language-survey subagent inherited a compacted summary written from the main agent's perspective and briefly mistook that summary for permission to coordinate the ongoing experiment. Its own actual authorization was only a bounded, read-only architecture survey.

Before identifying the mistake, it read the already-public practice summary, existing Git status and history, tracked-file names, and SHA-256 fingerprints of the existing objective, native sources, passing C evidence, original independence auditor, and **public** unopened final-benchmark manifest. It attempted to poll two already-finished process identifiers; neither process existed and neither invocation performed a measurement. It contacted existing correctness and chart reviewers, but did not authorize, run, duplicate, or modify their work.

The survey subagent immediately disclosed the incident to the main agent. The main agent stopped and quarantined it, instructed the original reviewers to accept task authority only from the main agent, and preserved the original single full correctness campaign. The survey subagent then disclosed its exact read-only paths and commands and released its slot.

No final opening, opening contents, hidden case, marker, unsealed final result, candidate timing, native build, package installation, source mutation, audit rule, or benchmark generation was accessed or performed by the survey subagent. Its access to the committed final-manifest fingerprint was still outside its assigned task and is recorded here rather than omitted. It must not be assigned any final-benchmark, opening, verifier, or candidate-freeze work.
