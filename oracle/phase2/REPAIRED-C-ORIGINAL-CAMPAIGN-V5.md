**not** identify the resulting private build root, either phase's native
binary, or their device, inode, mode, and SHA-256. Importantly, C15 and C16
both use the `rebar-phase2-native-build-v8-c-` root prefix. The old C15
controller's hard-coded root cannot identify C16. A prefix, a complete variant
source, a sealed archive hash, or an unverified caller-supplied path cannot
prove which native binary was built.

bind that new build to its exact root owner and to both independent
